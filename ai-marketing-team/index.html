The problem: marketing without an owner

GetMarketing does the work a small team would do: it diagnoses your website, fixes what costs you visibility and keeps building the pages and posts that bring enquiries from Google, AI answers and LinkedIn. Your job is to approve.

Most small companies don't lack ideas. They lack someone who owns marketing. Agencies are expensive, freelancers come and go, and AI tools still need someone to operate them. Meanwhile visibility quietly drops after a relaunch, or the website is found only by people who already know the brand name.
