What an AI marketing agency does differently

A classic agency sells hours. An AI marketing agency sells results, because AI takes over the repetitive work: analysing data, finding problems, writing pages, reporting. That makes it faster and much cheaper.

What matters most is not the AI, but the order of work. We don't start with an editorial calendar, we start with a diagnosis. Often the real problem isn't missing content but the structure of the website.
