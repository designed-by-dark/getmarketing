## What is a virtual CMO?

A virtual CMO (vCMO) is a senior marketing leader who works for you remotely instead of as a full-time executive. Typically they set strategy and direction, then rely on your team, or on agencies, to execute.

That last step is where most small companies get stuck. There is no team. The roadmap sits in a folder, and nothing happens. GetMarketing closes that gap. Strategy and execution live in the same system, so the plan turns into published pages instead of slides.
