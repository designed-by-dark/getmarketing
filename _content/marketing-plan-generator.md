## What makes a good marketing plan?

- **Based on data, not templates.** Real search demand and real competitors.
- **Prioritised.** Three things done well beat ten things started.
- **Concrete.** Named pages and posts, not "do more content".
- **Measurable.** A baseline and a few KPIs that matter for enquiries.
- **Executable with your resources.** A plan that needs a team you don't have isn't a plan.
