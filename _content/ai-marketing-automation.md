## What AI marketing automation is

Classic marketing automation runs rules: if a visitor does X, send message Y. It needs someone to set those rules, and it assumes you already have traffic and leads to work with.

AI marketing automation goes a step earlier. It reads your data, decides what is worth doing and does the work itself. For most small and mid-sized companies the biggest gap isn't email workflows. It's that too few of the right people find the website at all. That is where GetMarketing starts.

## What GetMarketing automates

- **The diagnosis:** visibility over time, ranking losses, brand vs. non-brand traffic, technical errors and untapped search demand, all from real data
- **Prioritisation:** an action plan ranked by impact, with expected searches per page
- **Technical fixes:** redirects after relaunches, category pages instead of filter URLs, titles, duplicates, internal links
- **Content production:** category pages, guides and pages built to be cited in AI answers, published after approval
- **LinkedIn drafts:** for the company and management. You post them yourself, so no bot behaviour.
- **Monitoring and reporting:** alerts when visibility drops, plus a monthly report on what went live, what ranks and which enquiries came in

Email nurturing, paid ads and custom AI workflows are handled by our Enterprise team when you need them.
