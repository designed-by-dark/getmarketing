## What is AI in marketing?

AI in marketing means using artificial intelligence for marketing tasks: analysis, planning, copy, images, personalisation and reporting. The value depends less on the model than on what the AI works with: real data, your expertise and your approval.

## How AI is used in marketing

- **Analysis:** evaluating search data, competitors and your website in minutes instead of days.
- **Content:** copy, guides and posts that fit your brand.
- **AI visibility (GEO):** structuring content so ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews cite it.
- **Personalisation:** content for different audiences and stages.
- **Chatbots and service:** answers to common questions around the clock.

## Where marketing AI fails

- **Generic output:** without context, every text sounds the same.
- **Work comes back to you:** you become the editor of dozens of drafts.
- **Credits as a black box:** every regeneration costs, even when the AI got it wrong.
- **Volume instead of impact:** lots of posts, but nobody searches for them.
