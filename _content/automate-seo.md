## What it means to automate SEO

Automating SEO means letting software handle the repeatable parts of search engine optimisation: collecting data, finding problems, prioritising, producing pages and reporting. You make the decisions that need your knowledge of the business.

Good SEO automation doesn't publish hundreds of thin pages. Google treats mass-produced content as spam ("scaled content abuse"), and it damages the whole domain. Automation should make you faster at the right things, not louder.

## How GetMarketing automates SEO

**1. We pull the data.** Your Search Console, plus search volumes and rankings from SISTRIX, Semrush and DataForSEO.

**2. We diagnose.** Where visibility dropped and when. How much traffic comes only from your brand. Which pages compete with each other, and which buying searches nobody on your site answers.

**3. We fix the structure.** Redirects, category pages instead of filter URLs, titles and H1s with the product type, duplicates and internal links.

**4. We prioritise.** Every new page gets a target term, expected searches and a priority, sorted by impact rather than by volume.

**5. We build the pages.** Category pages, guides and pages built to be cited in AI answers, compared against the pages that currently rank.

**6. You approve, it goes live.** Approved pages are published in your shop or CMS.

**7. We watch it.** If your visibility drops, you get an alert. Every month you see what went live, what ranks and which enquiries came in.

## What you can automate, and what you shouldn't

| Automate | Keep a human on it |
|---|---|
| Keyword research and clustering | Which offers you want to push |
| Technical checks and fix suggestions | Approving changes to your site |
| Page and guide drafts | Facts only you know (prices, references) |
| Publishing and indexing | Backlinks and PR (these can't be automated well) |
| Monitoring and reporting | Final approval |
