What SaaS marketing actually takes

SaaS marketing is different from classic product marketing in three ways:

  • Buyers research before they talk to anyone. They compare tools, read reviews and increasingly ask ChatGPT for a shortlist.
  • The product changes constantly. Pages go stale fast.
  • Retention matters as much as acquisition. Content has to help existing users, not just attract new ones.

For a small team, the most reliable channel is usually organic search and AI answers: pages that keep bringing qualified visitors without a growing ad budget.

SaaS marketing FAQs

What is SaaS marketing?

Marketing for software sold as a subscription, with a focus on research-driven buyers, fast-changing products and retention.

Which channels work best for B2B SaaS?

For small teams: organic search, AI answers and founder-led LinkedIn. Ads work once you know what converts.
