What SaaS marketing actually takes

GetMarketing diagnoses your website, fixes what costs you visibility and builds the pages that buyers search for: comparisons, use cases, integrations and answers that AI assistants cite. You approve, and the system does the work.

SaaS marketing is different from classic product marketing in three ways:

Buyers research before they talk to anyone

They compare tools, read reviews and increasingly ask ChatGPT for a shortlist.

The product changes constantly

Pages go stale fast.

Retention matters as much as acquisition

Content has to help existing users, not just attract new ones.

For a small team, the most reliable channel is usually organic search and AI answers: pages that keep bringing qualified visitors without a growing ad budget.
