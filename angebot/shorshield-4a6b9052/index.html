Why now

DORA asks for strong authentication on four kinds of access

RTS 2024/1774, Art. 21(f), in force since 17 January 2025. Every piece of content points at these four.
1Remote access
2Privileged access
3Critical or important functions
4Publicly accessible systems
Fix found in your profile
Before posting starts
Now“DORA has phishing-resistant authentication requirements.”
Better“DORA requires strong authentication based on leading practices. In 2026, leading practice means phishing-resistant methods.”
WhyThe legal text never says phishing-resistant. A compliance reader checks it and stops trusting the rest.
