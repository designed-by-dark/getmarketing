Walego
★ Featured · GTM Automation B2B outreach · Global
Visit website ↗ Our profile on Walego

An AI system that runs your go-to-market.

Walego is an AI go-to-market platform that decides who to contact, what to say, and where to reach them, then runs personalized B2B outreach across LinkedIn, email, Instagram, WhatsApp and X, with a built-in CRM that classifies replies and tracks deals. Instead of blasting messages, it builds the sales strategy from your website, sources high-intent prospects, qualifies them against your ICP, and reports back so campaigns keep improving. Across 550+ client campaigns Walego averages a 31.1% reply rate, with top performers past 40%.

Best for

  • B2B sales teams that need predictable pipeline without manual prospecting
  • Lead-gen and outbound agencies delivering under their own brand
  • Founders and consultants selling considered products where targeting beats volume
  • Teams that want AI to decide who to reach, what to say, and where

What it does

  • AI-generated sales strategy from your website
  • Sourcing blocks that track public databases for high-intent signals
  • Automated ICP-based qualification before any message goes out
  • Personalized outreach across five channels
  • Built-in CRM with reply classification and deal tracking
