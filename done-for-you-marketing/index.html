What done-for-you marketing should actually mean

Done-for-you (DFY) marketing means someone else owns the work: strategy, execution and reporting. In practice, many DFY offers turn out to be one of two things. Either an agency that bills thousands a month and delivers two articles, or a tool that "does it for you" until you realise you're the editor.

Real done-for-you starts with a diagnosis, not a content calendar. It fixes structural problems before producing more content, and it reports in enquiries, not in posts published.

What is DFY marketing?
