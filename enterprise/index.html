FAQ

Questions we hear most

How is Enterprise different from the €249 plan?

It runs on the same system. With the software, the system does the work and checks quality automatically, and you approve. With Enterprise, a named person checks every text, takes on the technical work, including theme code and page speed, and is responsible for the result.

Do we have to commit for long?

The foundation sprint is a one-time project. Ongoing work has a minimum of 3 months, after that you can cancel monthly.

What access do you need?

For the diagnosis, read access to Google Search Console is enough. Write access to your CMS only comes later, when the first changes are approved. Everything is set up step by step.

Can you work with our developer or freelancer?

Yes. We brief your own freelancer or developer, and they keep doing the parts they already do.

Do you also run ads?

Yes, Google Ads and Google Shopping are available as an add-on in Enterprise. They are not part of the software.

Who is behind it?

Darya Kablash, with more than 8 years in B2B marketing in the DACH region and over 55 completed projects. Every Enterprise client works with her directly, not with juniors.
