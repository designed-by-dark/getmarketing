FAQ

Questions we hear most

Will our clients know GetMarketing is behind it?

No. Portal, content and reports run under your brand. The client relationship stays entirely with you.

What does it cost us?

You pay a fixed monthly purchase price per client project. You decide what you charge your clients. We discuss terms personally.

Who checks the quality?

Every draft goes through an automatic check for facts, brand and SEO. Then you approve, then the client. The system learns from every correction.

Which channels are included?

SEO articles, landing pages, social posts and reporting. More channels such as Google Business Profile or paid ads are possible on request.

Where is the data stored?

Hosted at Hetzner in Germany, GDPR-compliant. Each client gets their own environment.
