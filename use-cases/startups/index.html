What we often see

Where young companies usually get stuck

  • Only the name gets found. One SaaS startup ranked in the top 100 for exactly one search term, with almost zero visitors from Google
  • Content for the wrong term. 20 people a month search for the problem “knowledge loss”, 2,400 for the solution “knowledge management”. Without search data it is easy to write past the market
  • Marketing happens on the side. Founders squeeze content in between product, sales and fundraising, and after three weeks the blog goes quiet again
  • One person is expected to do everything. Research, articles, LinkedIn, website and reporting at the same time is too much for anyone, even a good marketer
  • Nobody sees what works. Without reporting, it stays unclear which post brought enquiries and which did not

FAQ

Questions we hear most

We already have someone for marketing. Is it still worth it?

Especially then. The system handles research, drafts, publishing and reporting. Your marketer sets the topics, approves and has time for customer calls, partnerships and events.

€249 is a lot for us. Does it pay off?

At 12 articles a month that is about €21 per article, including LinkedIn drafts and the report. If a customer is worth more than €2,490 a year to you, a single extra customer pays for the whole year.

How fast will we see results?

The diagnosis and first fixes come right away. New rankings and enquiries usually build over 3 to 6 months. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings.

Won’t it sound like AI?

The system writes from your material and learns your terms and no-gos from every correction. Nothing goes live without your approval.

What do we need to start?

Your website, read access to Google Search Console and later access to your CMS. Target groups and existing content help to get the tone right quickly.
