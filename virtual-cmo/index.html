What is a virtual CMO?

A virtual CMO (vCMO) is a senior marketing leader who works for you remotely instead of as a full-time executive. Typically they set strategy and direction, then rely on your team, or on agencies, to execute.

That last step is where most small companies get stuck. There is no team. The roadmap sits in a folder, and nothing happens. GetMarketing closes that gap. Strategy and execution live in the same system, so the plan turns into published pages instead of slides.

What your virtual CMO does

  • Diagnosis: visibility over time, losses after relaunches, how much of your traffic is only your brand, and which buying searches your competitors win.
  • Priorities: a clear action plan. Fixes first, then new pages, each with expected search demand.
  • Fixes: redirects, category pages, titles, duplicates, internal links.
  • Content that gets found: category pages, guides and pages built for AI answers, published in your shop or CMS after approval.
  • LinkedIn for management: drafts in your voice. You post them yourself.
  • Monitoring: an alert when visibility drops, plus a monthly report in business language.

Virtual CMO FAQs

What is a virtual CMO?

A marketing leader who works for you remotely instead of full-time. With GetMarketing, strategy and execution come together.

What is the difference between a virtual and a fractional CMO?

The terms overlap. Both mean senior marketing leadership without a full-time hire. The real difference is whether someone also does the work, and with GetMarketing that is included.

How much does a virtual CMO cost?

Human fractional CMOs often cost €3,000 or more a month before execution. GetMarketing costs €249 per month per domain, everything included.

When does a virtual CMO make sense?

When marketing needs direction and consistent execution, but a full-time hire isn't justified yet.
