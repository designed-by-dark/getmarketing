The result counts, not the technology

Many people juggle a dozen AI tools, learn prompting and still get mediocre copy. With GetMarketing you don't operate tools or write prompts. You get finished work: corrected pages, new guides, LinkedIn drafts and a report that shows what it achieved.

What your AI marketing manager takes over

Diagnosis. Where did you lose visibility, how much traffic comes only from your brand name, and for which buying-intent searches do only your competitors win?

Priorities. An action plan sorted by impact: fixes first, then new pages, each with its expected searches.

Technical fixes. Redirects after relaunches, category pages instead of filter URLs, titles with the generic term, duplicates, internal links.

Pages and guides. Written in your tone of voice and published to your shop or CMS only after your approval.

AI visibility. Content that ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews cite as a source.

LinkedIn. Drafts for the company and leadership that you post yourself.

Monitoring and report. Alerts when visibility drops, and every month: what went live, what ranks, which enquiries came in.
