What is an AI CMO?

An AI CMO is a system that takes on the job a chief marketing officer would own: deciding what matters, getting it done and checking whether it worked. A real AI CMO doesn't stop at writing text on request. It starts with a diagnosis of your business, sets priorities and delivers the work.

GetMarketing is built around that idea, with one deliberate focus: being found. That means Google, AI answers like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, and LinkedIn. It is the channel mix where small and mid-sized companies usually lose the most, and where the work compounds over time.

What your AI CMO does, step by step

1. Diagnosis. Connect your website and Search Console. GetMarketing analyses your visibility with real search data. Where did you lose rankings? How much of your traffic comes only from your brand name? Which searches with buying intent find nobody but your competitors?

2. Priorities. From the diagnosis comes an action plan: technical fixes first, then new pages and posts, each with expected searches and a priority. Like a good CMO, it tells you what not to do as well.

3. Fixes. Redirects after a relaunch, category pages instead of filter URLs, product titles without a product type, duplicates between pages. These structural problems often cost more visibility than any lack of content.

4. Pages and posts. The system writes category pages, guides, content for AI answers and LinkedIn drafts for management. Approved pages are published in your shop or CMS. You post LinkedIn content yourself, so nothing looks like a bot.

5. Monitoring and report. If your visibility drops, you get an alert. Every month you see what went live, what ranks and which enquiries came in.
