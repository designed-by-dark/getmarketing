What is marketing automation?

Marketing automation means letting software run repetitive marketing tasks automatically. Typical examples are email sequences, lead scoring, segmentation and reporting. The goal is to turn prospects into customers with less manual work.

How does marketing automation work?

Classically, by rules: if a contact does X, Y happens. For example, someone downloads a whitepaper and receives a case study three days later. That needs a CRM, clean data and someone to set up and maintain the sequences.

AI-powered automation goes further. It analyses data, decides what makes sense and does part of the work itself.

Real-world examples

  • Welcome sequence: new contacts receive a series with the most important information.
  • Lead nurturing: content matched to the stage a prospect is currently in.
  • Re-engagement: inactive contacts receive a targeted campaign.
  • Visibility monitoring: an alert as soon as rankings drop. GetMarketing does this.
  • Content production: pages and guides based on real search data. GetMarketing does this too.

What GetMarketing automates

  • Visibility diagnosis: losses, brand share, technical errors, untapped demand
  • Prioritisation by impact, with expected searches per page
  • Technical fixes: redirects, category pages, titles, duplicates, internal links
  • Pages and guides, published only after your approval
  • Content for AI answers (GEO) and LinkedIn drafts that you post yourself
  • Monitoring and a monthly report with rankings and enquiries

Email sequences, CRM workflows and custom automation are handled by our Enterprise team when you need them.

Key marketing automation KPIs

  • Enquiries or leads per month and their source
  • Non-brand traffic from organic search
  • Conversion rate from visit to enquiry
  • Cost per enquiry
  • Open and click rates for email sequences

Challenges

  • Too little traffic: without visitors, every sequence runs into nothing.
  • Generic content: automated copy without context feels interchangeable.
  • Maintenance: many systems fail because nobody internally has the time or IT capacity.
  • Silent failure: planned content doesn't go out and nobody notices.

GetMarketing answers this with an onboarding questionnaire, your approval for every piece and a status that always shows what went live. You don't have to run anything.

How to get started

  1. First check whether enough prospects arrive. The diagnosis shows it.
  2. Fix structural errors before creating new content.
  3. Build pages for searches with buying intent.
  4. Only then set up sequences for the contacts who come in.
