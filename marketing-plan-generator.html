Further reading

What makes a good AI marketing plan?

A marketing plan fails when it stays a document. Most founders have written one: or had an agency write one: and then never looked at it again. The real problem is the gap between planning and execution.

An effective AI marketing plan does three things: it identifies the right channels for your specific business and audience, it sets a realistic execution schedule that doesn't depend on you having three spare hours a week, and it tells you how to know if it's working.

GetMarketing generates plans based on live data (your website, your competitors, your market) and then closes the loop by handing execution directly to agents that run the work. The plan and the execution are the same system.

Who is an AI marketing plan generator for?

Founders and small business owners who handle marketing themselves: or who have tried agencies and freelancers without getting consistent results. If you know marketing should be happening but you can't make it happen consistently, a system that runs from a plan is more useful than another template.

Marketingplan erstellen: was gehört dazu?

Ein vollständiger Marketingplan enthält: eine Kanalstrategie (wo du aktiv sein solltest und warum), einen Content-Plan (was du produzierst und in welcher Frequenz), eine SEO-Roadmap (welche Keywords du angreifst), eine Wettbewerbsanalyse und ein Tracking-Setup, das dir zeigt, was funktioniert. GetMarketing erstellt alle diese Bestandteile automatisch: und setzt sie dann auch um.
