What is an AI SEO tool?

An AI SEO tool uses artificial intelligence for search engine optimisation tasks such as keyword research, analysis, writing, technical checks and reporting. There are tools for single tasks, for example research, copy or audits, and systems that take over the whole process.

GetMarketing is the second kind. You don't get yet another dashboard telling you what to do. The work gets done.

How AI is changing search engine optimisation

  • Faster: research and drafts take minutes instead of days.
  • AI search: ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews cite pages that are clear, factual and well structured (GEO).
  • The volume risk: Google treats mass-produced, generic pages as spam ("scaled content abuse"). Quality matters more than quantity.

How the GetMarketing AI SEO tool works

  1. Collect data: your Search Console plus search volume and rankings from SISTRIX, Semrush and DataForSEO
  2. Diagnosis: When did visibility drop? How high is the brand share? Which pages compete with each other, and which buying searches go unanswered?
  3. Fix the structure: redirects, category pages, titles and H1, duplicates, internal links
  4. Prioritise: every new page gets a target term, expected searches and a priority.
  5. Build pages: category pages, guides and pages for AI answers, compared with the pages that rank today
  6. Approve and publish: in your shop or CMS
  7. Monitor: alerts when visibility drops and a monthly report with rankings and enquiries

Frequently asked questions about AI SEO

What is AI SEO?

Search engine optimisation where AI handles research, analysis, drafts and reporting.

Can ChatGPT write SEO copy?

Yes, but without search data, diagnosis and expertise you usually get generic copy nobody searches for.

Does Google detect AI content?

Google judges helpfulness, not the tool. Specific, checked pages rank. Generic mass content doesn't.
