What is AI blog writing?

AI blog writing means using AI to research, outline and write blog posts. Done well, it saves a writer days of work per article. Done badly, it fills your blog with generic posts that no one searches for, and that Google increasingly ignores.

The difference isn't the AI model. It's what goes into it: real search data, your expertise, your tone and your approval before anything goes live.

How GetMarketing writes your blog

  1. Gap analysis. Which searches with buying intent bring your competitors visitors, but not you? Which questions do Google and AI answers show for your topics?
  2. Plan. Every post gets a target term, expected searches and a reason to exist. No filler topics.
  3. Draft in your voice. Built from your onboarding answers and materials, with your real examples, not generic ones.
  4. Review. You read the draft, add what only you know and correct anything that doesn't sound like you.
  5. Approve and publish. One click, and the post goes live in your CMS.
  6. Measure. The monthly report shows which posts rank and bring enquiries, and that feeds the next plan.

Blog posts are only one part

A blog alone rarely fixes visibility. GetMarketing also:

  • fixes technical problems that hold your rankings back (redirects, titles, duplicates)
  • builds category and landing pages for buying searches, not just guides
  • writes content built to be cited in AI answers like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews
  • turns approved posts into LinkedIn drafts for management

FAQs

Can AI write a whole blog post?

Yes. The real question is whether anyone will search for it and whether it says something specific. That's why GetMarketing plans topics from search data and checks every post.

Will Google penalise AI content?

Google penalises unhelpful content, especially at scale, not AI as such. Specific, checked posts that answer real searches rank.

Can I control tone and brand voice?

Yes. Through onboarding, your materials and your edits. What you correct once doesn't come back.

Does it publish to WordPress?
