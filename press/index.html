About GetMarketing

GetMarketing is an AI marketing platform for B2B companies in the DACH region. It builds a company's marketing strategy, creates the content and publishes it on a consistent schedule every week, using multi-agent workflows across SEO, content and LinkedIn. Founders review what goes out instead of hiring a full marketing team.

GetMarketing was founded in 2025 by growth consultant Darya Kablash and is part of Batch 2 of the Young Founders Network incubator.
