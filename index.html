How it works

A loop that learns every week

Every week your marketing runs through the same four steps. What the measuring shows feeds into the next plan, so the strategy keeps adjusting.

Every weekre-planned
1Plan

The Strategy Agent knows your business, your market and your numbers and decides what gets made this week.

2Create

SEO, content and design agents write articles and posts based on real search data. An experienced marketer reviews every draft.

3Approve

You read each piece and approve it with one click. It is then published automatically.

4Measure

GA4 and Search Console show which pieces bring visitors and inquiries. That feeds into the next plan.

Your part

Set the goals and approve the pieces. The system handles the rest, from keyword research to the weekly report.
