FAQ

Questions we hear most

Do I have to write the content myself, or do I just get a plan?

You write nothing yourself. GetMarketing writes the pages and posts, checks every draft automatically for facts, brand and SEO, and puts it in front of you. You approve with one click and the page goes live in your CMS.

Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude?

ChatGPT answers what you ask. You still have to know what to do and set aside time for it every week, and that is usually what is missing. GetMarketing decides from real search data what comes next, does it on a schedule, publishes in your CMS, measures the result, warns you when visibility drops and learns from your corrections.

Is this all of my marketing?

No, and on purpose. We cover the channels that proved to be the most important 20% in our projects: Google, AI answers and LinkedIn. More channels would mean more decisions and more approvals for you, the opposite of the relief you want from us.

Do you make videos?

No. No client has asked for it, AI video is exactly the generic output people complain about, and a video stops working long before a page that ranks. LinkedIn posts come with a finished visual.

Do you run paid ads?

Not in the software, because that means responsibility for your ad budget and access to your ad accounts. With Enterprise, Google Ads and Google Shopping are available as an add-on.

Does it change code on my website, or only text?

We change content and SEO settings through your CMS, not your theme code: new pages and posts, titles, meta descriptions, headings, alt texts, redirects and schema markup on new pages. Every change shows before and after and can be undone with one click. Theme code, page speed and robots.txt come as a ready brief for your developer, or we do it for you with Enterprise.

Which systems does it work with?

WordPress and Shopify at launch, with direct publishing. TYPO3 and Webflow follow later. For any other CMS you get every page as HTML or Markdown to paste in. Data comes from Google Search Console. LinkedIn posts you copy and post yourself, nothing is posted automatically.

When will I see results?

The analysis and the first fixes come right away. More traffic and enquiries from new pages usually take 3 to 6 months, because Google needs time to trust new pages. The monthly report shows what is live, what ranks and which enquiries came in, so you see early what works.

Is there a cheaper plan for small companies or startups?

No. €249 a month per domain, or €2,490 a year with two months free. Our costs depend on domains and pages, not on headcount, so there is no plan by company size. If you have no revenue yet, start with the free marketing plan generator.

Can I cancel, and what happens to my content?

You can cancel monthly. Everything that was published belongs to you and stays online.
